# The Recipe Clause — Part 1: "Not Your Job"

Long-form, ~9 min · 9:16 or 16:9 · original story (family-business, malicious-compliance trope)

**Cast**
- **Aria** (29) — heroine. Head chef in all but title; officially "Kitchen Operations Manager". Calm, sharp, never raises her voice. *Look: white rolled-sleeve shirt, black tailored trousers, black stilettos, sleek dark hair.*
- **Vanessa** (32) — stepsister, new CEO. Loud glam, never cooked. *Platinum hair, black leather, red heels.*
- **Daniel** (55) — stepdad, founder of "Harlow & Daughter", just retired. *Silver beard, navy suit.*
- **Leo** (24) — sous-chef, Aria's ally. *Ginger hair, glasses — chef whites in the kitchen.*
- **Maya** (34) — front-of-house manager, Aria's friend. *Mustard jacket.*

**Format key** — `NARR` = Aria's voice-over (edge-tts) · `🎬` = shot for MiniMax (≤5s, chest-up unless noted) · `⚡` = villain reaction shot (2–3s close-up, mandatory after every punchline)

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:25)

🎬 Close-up: Aria folding a white apron into a perfect square, sliding it across a steel counter.
🎬 Close-up: Vanessa staring at the apron, red nails hovering over it.

**NARR:** Eight years. Forty-one recipes. Three restaurants built on my food. And it took my stepsister exactly four minutes to decide I was overpaid.

🎬 Title card: **THE RECIPE CLAUSE — PART 1: NOT YOUR JOB**

---

## SCENE 1 — The Handover (0:25–1:40)
*Restaurant dining room, morning, staff gathered.*

🎬 Medium: Daniel, champagne glass raised, proud.

**DANIEL:** Thirty years ago, I opened one little kitchen with a borrowed oven. Today, Harlow and Daughter has three locations and a waiting list. And from Monday… my daughter Vanessa runs it.

🎬 Medium: Vanessa steps forward, beaming, red heels clicking.
🎬 Close-up: Aria clapping politely, face unreadable.

**NARR:** Daughter. Singular. I've been in this family since I was eleven. I've been in this kitchen since I was twenty-one. I'm still "the stepdaughter who cooks."

**VANESSA:** Thank you, Dad. I promise you — Harlow and Daughter is about to get a lot more… *efficient.*

⚡ Leo glances at Aria. Aria doesn't look back.

---

## SCENE 2 — The Cut (1:40–3:10)
*Back office. Vanessa behind Daniel's old desk, tablet in hand. Aria standing.*

**VANESSA:** I went through the payroll last night. Interesting reading. *(swipes)* "Recipe development bonus." Two thousand a month. Since when?

**ARIA:** Since your dad tasted my short rib and tore up the old menu. Eight years ago.

**VANESSA:** Cute. But your contract says Kitchen Operations Manager. Rotas, ordering, health inspections. Nothing about "inventing." So — your recipe bonus is cancelled. You were hired to manage shifts, not play chef.

🎬 Aria unties her apron, folds it, sets it neatly on the desk in front of Vanessa.

**ARIA:** Bold move, cutting the only person here who can cook. Fine. I'll manage shifts. You manage the menu… good luck reading it.

⚡ Vanessa's smile freezes. She pushes the apron away with one finger.

**VANESSA:** Relax. Anyone can follow a recipe.

**ARIA:** Anyone can follow one. Somebody had to *write* it. You weren't hired for that either, remember?

⚡ Vanessa opens her mouth. Closes it. Taps her tablet too hard.

**VANESSA:** Close the door on your way out.

**ARIA:** *(opening it wider)* It's a kitchen, Vanessa. The door stays open. Heat has to go somewhere.

⚡ Vanessa's jaw tightens as the door swings.

---

## SCENE 3 — The Rule (3:10–4:10)
*Kitchen, evening. Aria pins a laminated sheet to the fridge.*

🎬 Close-up: the sheet — "KITCHEN OPERATIONS MANAGER — DUTIES" (text blurred, unreadable).

**LEO:** You're actually doing this.

**ARIA:** I'm doing my job. Exactly my job.

**LEO:** And the fermented base? The one in every sauce? You make that every Sunday.

**ARIA:** At home. On my own time. With my own recipe.
*(beat)*
Sundays aren't on the rota, Leo.

⚡ Leo slowly grins.

**NARR:** Here's what nobody in that office understood. My dishes weren't recipes. They were a system. And the heart of the system lived in a jar on my kitchen counter — not theirs.

---

## SCENE 4 — Day Three (4:10–5:40)
*Kitchen, dinner rush. Steam, noise, plates coming back.*

🎬 Medium: a cook tastes a sauce, winces.
🎬 Close-up: Maya pushes through the swing doors with two returned plates.

**MAYA:** Table six says the short rib tastes "like a different restaurant." Table nine just asked if we changed owners.

**MAYA:** *(beat)* …I didn't know what to tell them.

🎬 Medium: Vanessa storms in, phone in hand.

**VANESSA:** Aria. Fix this. Now.

**ARIA:** *(checking a clipboard)* Not in my job description. You laminated it. *(nods)* It's on the fridge.

⚡ Vanessa glances at the fridge. The sheet is there. Her jaw tightens.

**VANESSA:** You're being petty.

**ARIA:** No. Petty is cutting my pay to lease yourself a new car. I'm being *accurate.*

⚡ The kitchen goes silent. Leo drops a spoon. Vanessa's cheeks flush red.

**VANESSA:** *(low)* That's company business.

**ARIA:** So is the food. Funny how you only read half the file.

---

## SCENE 5 — Daniel (5:40–6:50)
*Restaurant terrace, night. Daniel and Aria alone.*

**DANIEL:** Reviews dropped half a star in three days. Vanessa says you're sabotaging her.

**ARIA:** I'm doing exactly what she asked. To the letter.

**DANIEL:** Aria… come on. It's family.

**ARIA:** Then family can pay me like staff. Or lose me like staff.

⚡ Daniel looks through the window at Vanessa, laughing on her phone. She doesn't notice him.

**DANIEL:** *(quietly)* I signed something, years ago. About the recipes.

**ARIA:** What did you sign?

**DANIEL:** *(standing, avoiding her eyes)* Ask me when this is over.

**NARR:** He walked away before I could ask again. Daniel has never once walked away from a conversation. That's when I knew the paper mattered.

---

## SCENE 6 — The Replacement (6:50–8:30)
*Kitchen, next morning. Staff lined up. A man in a designer chef's jacket beside Vanessa (an extra — face can be off-camera or back to camera).*

**VANESSA:** Everyone, meet Chef Laurent. Two TV shows. A cookbook. A *real* chef. He'll be modernising our menu.

🎬 Medium: Vanessa turns to Aria, savouring it.

**VANESSA:** You're replaceable, Aria. Everyone is.

**ARIA:** *(already walking toward the door)* Everyone's replaceable. Ask him who wrote the recipes he's about to ruin.

⚡ Close-up: Vanessa's confident smile drains, one second at a time.

🎬 Medium: Maya's phone buzzes. She reads it — eyes widening.

**MAYA:** Vanessa… the booking system. "Party of one. Saturday the 18th." The name is… Elise Marchetti.

⚡ Vanessa freezes. The colour leaves her face.

**LEO:** *(whispering to Aria)* The critic? The one who closed Bianco's with one review?

**ARIA:** *(not looking back)* She ordered my short rib last time. Three years ago. Called it "the reason to visit the city."

---

## CLIFFHANGER (8:30–9:00)

🎬 Close-up: Aria in her own small apartment kitchen, lifting the lid off a jar. Steam. A faint smile.

**NARR:** Fourteen days until the most important table in our history. A TV chef with no recipe. A CEO with no idea. And a jar on my counter that nobody at Harlow and Daughter even knew existed.

🎬 Close-up: Vanessa alone in the dark office, staring at the folded white apron on the desk.

**NARR:** She wanted efficient. She's about to get it.

🎬 Text card: **PART 2 — "THE CRITIC'S TABLE"**

## Production notes

- **Speaking lines:** the series uses dialogue, so either (a) narrate everything in Aria's voice and show reactions (cheapest, works with the current pipeline), or (b) give each character an edge-tts voice (Vanessa: brighter, faster; Daniel: older, slower; Aria: low, calm) and cut to the speaker. (b) feels more like the reference format.
- **Shot count:** ~22 character shots, ~10 of them ⚡ reaction close-ups — single-face, 2–3s, the cheapest and most reliable shots for MiniMax.
- **Sets needed (Flow):** restaurant dining room, back office, professional kitchen, terrace at night, small apartment kitchen.
- **Props:** folded white apron, laminated duty sheet (blurred), jar with lid, phone with booking screen (blurred).
- **Captions:** burn the ⚡ punchlines bigger than the rest — they're the moments people screenshot and share.
- **Before Part 1 goes out:** make Aria's reference set (base prompt in the conversation; add her as a tracker card).
